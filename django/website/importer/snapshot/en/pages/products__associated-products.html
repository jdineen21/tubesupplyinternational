Associated Products

Here at Heat Ex Tube Limited, we don’t only stock high-quality heat exchanger tubes to the process industry, we also supply an extensive range of products to ensure systems function correctly and efficiently – including forged header plugs and pressed support boxes.

Forged header plugs

Used in air-cooled heat exchangers to seal the ends of piping systems, our forged header plugs or stainless steel plugs are ideal for the petrochemical and power industry.

They offer:

  • high strength
  • lightweight
  • precision
  • long-lifespan


Pressed support boxes

Air cooled exchangers have a bundle of finned tubes with rectangular box headers on both ends of the tube. Pressed support boxes are used to distribute fluids from the piping to the tubes and fully support finned tubes according to API 661.

Get a quote for forged header plugs/pressed support boxes

Manufactured in aluminium, brass, copper and galvanised steel, we supply pressed support boxes as half boxes or in rectangular/hexagonal formats to suit the finned tube pitch.

Forged header plugs are available in carbon steel (ASTM A105) and stainless steel (A182), as well as nickel alloys and titanium.

Whether it be stainless steel plugs or other forged plugs, for a quote please email specified quantities, thread sizes and threaded lengths to tubesales@heatextube.co.uk. We can cater for unusual sizes if drawings are provided.

The more information you can give us about required pressed support boxes and stainless steel plugs – the more accurately we can price our products.
