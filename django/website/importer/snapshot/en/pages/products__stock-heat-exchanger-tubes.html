Heat Exchanger Tubes & Boiler Tubes For Heat Exchangers - HETL

Heat Ex Tube Limited are leading stockists of over 1,400 tons of quality heat exchanger tubes and boiler tubes - in lengths of up to 19.6 meters.

Heat exchanger tubes are used to cool, heat or reheat liquids and gases - making them perfect for:

  • Chemical processing
  • Engine cooling
  • Hydrocarbon processing
  • Offshore production
  • Oil refining
  • Nuclear power generation
