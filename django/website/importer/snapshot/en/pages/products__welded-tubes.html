Welded Tubes

If you’re looking for top quality welded tubes, you’ve come to the right place.

Here at Heat Ex Tube Limited, we have an extensive range of welded stainless steel, nickel alloy and titanium tubes available for you to choose from - including direct welded, welded or cold welded tubes, all manufactured to the highest quality and rigorously tested.

Our welded tubes are supplied in a range of sizes, starting from 12.7mm and ranging right the way through to 101.6mm OD, and are in straight or U bent form.

Welded tube specifications

We supply a wide range of ASTM/ASME welded tubes, including the following material grades:

A249 / A269 / A789 / B515 / B516 / B626 / B674 / B704 / B730 / B338

Equivalents, such as BS, DIN, ANFOR, UNI and JLS are also available on special request - so if you can’t see what you’re looking for, don’t hesitate to get in touch with us.

Grades of welded tubes available

  • Austenitic stainless grades: 304 / 304L / 304LN / 304H - 316 / 316L / 316H - 317 / 317L - 309S / 309H - 310S / 310H - 321 / 321H - 347 / 347H
  • Super Austenitic: 6 MOLY, 825 / 904 / 800 / 800H / NO8811
  • Duplex Grades: DUPLEX 2205 (S31803) / DUPLEX 2304 / SUPER DUPLEX 2507 (S32750)
  • Nickel Alloys: ALLOY 20 / 200 / 201 / 276 / 400 / 600 / 22 / 625 / 686 / 59
  • Super Ferritic's / Ferritic's: 29 - 4C / S44627 / 439 / 444 / 446
  • Titanium Grades: grade 2 / grade 3 / grade 7 / grade 12
